Welcome to Thandra Self Drive Cars (“Thandra,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled safely, transparently, and responsibly. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your data when you use the Thandra mobile application and our vehicle reservation services.

1. Information We Collect

To facilitate self-drive vehicle reservations, verify driver credentials under statutory transport laws, ensure fleet security, and prevent vehicle theft or absconding, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Identification & Contact Details

  • Full Name, Phone Number, and Permanent Residential Address: Collected for customer identity verification, reservation OTP validation, booking confirmation, and rental agreement generation.
  • Emergency Reference Contacts: Name and phone number of close personal references (such as family members, spouse, or guarantors) provided during booking or finance processing.

B. Government-Issued Identity & Driving Credentials

  • Driving License (DL): Cross-verified to validate legal eligibility to drive motor vehicles under the Indian Motor Vehicles Act.
  • Aadhaar & PAN Numbers: Collected for mandatory identity KYC verification, rental contract execution, and Zero-Damage Escrow authorization.

C. Reservation & Itinerary Data

  • Selected vehicle model, vehicle registration plate, pickup branch hub (Madhapur, Dilshuknagar, B.N reddy nagar, JNTU), destination points, pickup date/time, and return schedule.

D. Financing & Payment Assistance Inquiries (Where Applicable)

  • Loan reference details, banking partners, product specifications, and desired finance amount for users applying for rental vehicle financing assistance.

E. Device & Media Permissions

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used strictly to allow customers to upload vehicle inspection photos (pre-trip and post-trip damage checks) and verification documents.
  • Device Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): Subject to explicit affirmative consent as described below.

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policy on Sensitive Permissions and User Data:

  • Why Access is Requested: Thandra requests access to your device contacts strictly to allow you to conveniently select emergency contact references for vehicle reservation safety verification, emergency roadside assistance, and customer outreach during an active rental.
  • Prior In-App Disclosure: Before any permission request is triggered, our app presents a clear, prominent disclosure modal explaining the contact access purpose. Access is only requested upon your affirmative consent.
  • Strict Prohibition on Marketing & Sale: We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, trade, or share your contact data with third-party advertisers, marketing networks, or data brokers. Contact details are strictly restricted to emergency security verification.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate operational, security, and statutory purposes:

  • Reservation Fulfillment: To process, confirm, allocate vehicles, and manage rental schedules across our branch hubs.
  • Fraud Prevention & Fleet Security: To verify driving eligibility, prevent unauthorized vehicle handover, and protect assets from theft or misuse.
  • Emergency Assistance: To reach designated emergency references in the event of an accident, road breakdown, or urgent safety incident.
  • Legal & Statutory Compliance: To comply with statutory motor vehicle laws, police verification requirements, and taxation regulations.

4. Data Storage, Security & Cloud Safeguards

We implement enterprise-grade technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and backend services are encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols (HTTPS).
  • Cloud Storage: Customer records, booking logs, and verification credentials are securely stored in Google Cloud / Firebase enterprise infrastructure.
  • Access Controls: Database access is strictly restricted through role-based access control (RBAC) to authorized branch management personnel.

7. Data Retention Policy

Personal data is retained only for the duration necessary to complete your rental service, maintain necessary records under transport laws, or comply with statutory vehicle leasing audit requirements. Once the required retention period lapses or upon receipt of a verified deletion request, your data is permanently scrubbed.

Our vehicle reservation services are strictly restricted to individuals who possess a valid motor vehicle driving license and are at least 18 years of age. We do not knowingly solicit, process, or collect personal information from minors.
